Our Services for Garment & Spinning Partners

1. Waste-to-Fiber Conversion

We collect your pre-consumer cotton/polyester cutting waste and convert it into high-quality recycled fiber.

✅ Free pickup (Dhaka)
✅ Traceable output (GRS/GOTS-ready)
✅ 30–40% cheaper than virgin cotton

2. Zero-Waste Certification

Get official “Zero Textile Waste” certification for your factory—boosting your ESG score and buyer trust.

✅ Validated by 3rd-party audit
✅ Ready for H&M, Zara, Target compliance
✅ Annual partnership model

✅ Min. order: 5 MT
✅ 12-month price lock
✅ Blending support (up to 30% recycled)
