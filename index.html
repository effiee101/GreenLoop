GreenLoop Textiles Ltd.

1. Executive Summary

Core Business: Upcycling cutting room scraps and deadstock into high-quality fiber.

Location: Proximity to Economic Zones (Savar, Gazipur).

Legal Status: Private Limited Company (RJSC Registration).

Bangladesh is the world's leading green garment hub. We address the critical supply chain need for sustainable raw materials, leveraging GTF financing for rapid growth.
