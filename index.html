GreenLoop Textiles Ltd. is a private limited manufacturing company specializing in pre-consumer waste recycling.

Legal Structure

Type: Private Limited Company. Registration with RJSC initiated. Compliant with Bangladesh Labor Act 2006.

Business Nature

B2B Textile Manufacturing. Core focus is 'Green' or 'Circular' manufacturing, targeting large garment export houses and spinning mills.

USP

Advantage

Low-cost financing via Green Transformation Fund (GTF) for importing state-of-the-art machinery.

The total estimated project cost is approximately BDT 100,000,000 (USD 900k), primarily covered by the GTF Loan for CapEx.

Strategy: Product, Pricing, & Sales

Product/Services

  • Recycled Cotton Fiber: Primary output. Sold to Open-End spinning mills.
  • Recycled Polyester Yarn: Secondary output from plastic waste.
  • Zero-Waste Consulting: Value-added service to secure long-term raw material supply contracts with key garment factories.

Price Strategy

Adopt a Value-Based Pricing Model combined with Cost-Plus.

  • Recycled Fiber: Priced at 60-70% of virgin cotton market price, emphasizing cost savings and sustainability value to the buyer.
  • Consulting: Fee-based service tied to supply exclusivity.
  • Key Metric: Maintain 12-15% margin on cost.

Marketing & Sales

  • Direct Sales: Focus on securing MOUs with 5 large BGMEA member factories for raw material intake.
  • Digital Marketing: Target B2B buyers (Spinning/Weaving Mills) with compliance and sustainability data (traceability).
  • Certifications: Highlight GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard) and GRS (Global Recycled Standard) compliance.

Business Plan: November 2025

GreenLoop Textiles Ltd.

Specializing in industrial-scale upcycling of pre-consumer textile waste in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

1. Company & Business Type

Business Nature

B2B Circular Textile Manufacturing. We transform pre-consumer textile waste into high-quality recycled fiber (input) for spinning mills.

Legal Status: Private Limited Company. RJSC registered.

Core Value Proposition

  • Cost Reduction: Fiber priced 30-40% below virgin cotton.
  • Compliance: Provides traceable inputs for GOTS/GRS certified mills.
  • Scale: Targeting 2,500 MT annual processing volume (FY26).

2. Capital Investment & Structure

Primary Capital Source

BDT 500,000 Self-Investment

Founders' equity for immediate seed and start-up costs.

Primary Funding Source

GTF Loan (Green Transformation Fund) covers ~95% of BDT 100M CapEx for machinery (optical sorting, spinning line).

Funding Breakdown

  • CapEx: 95% (GTF Loan)
  • Equity: BDT 500K (Self-Invested)
  • Working Capital: Local bank facility (Post-launch)

4. Products & Price Strategy

Core Products

Recycled Cotton Fiber

Main output, sold to Open-End spinning mills.

Zero-Waste Consulting

Fee-based service ensuring raw material exclusivity.

Price Strategy: Value-Based

  • Fiber Pricing: Set at 60-70% of the virgin cotton market price.
  • Value Proposition: Attract large mills by guaranteeing cost savings and GRS/GOTS compliance.
  • Margin Target: Maintain a minimum of 12% - 15% Net Profit Margin.

5. Marketing & Sales Strategy

Raw Material Sourcing (Buy-Side)

  • Direct MOUs: Secure binding agreements with 5 large BGMEA factories for exclusive waste streams.
  • Service: Use Zero-Waste Consulting as an incentive for exclusivity.

Product Sales (Sell-Side)

  • Target: Open-End Spinning Mills focused on recycled blends.
  • Sales Channel: Direct B2B sales emphasizing traceability and sustainability certifications (GRS/GOTS).
